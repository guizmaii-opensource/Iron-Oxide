How it works

  1. 1 Ask your AI

    Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI assistant. It asks about your goals, how many days you can train and for how long, your equipment, your experience and any injuries, then writes the program in Iron Oxide's format.

  2. 2 Paste it into Iron Oxide

    Bring the program into the app and make it your active program. Iron Oxide checks it and points at anything it can't use. No AI at hand? Start from the built-in full-body 3-day barbell program.

  3. 3 Train

    Open the app at the gym and follow the day: big buttons, a rest timer, a plate calculator and automatic progression.

Fully personalised.

The program is yours: your days, your exercises, your notes, the movements you leave out. The app adapts each session to what you actually lifted.

Every exercise carries its own progression rule. Hit all your reps and the next session adds weight; work through a rep range and it adds reps first, then weight; fall short several sessions in a row and it takes some weight off so you can build back up.

Made for the gym floor

One hand, sweaty fingers, a phone on the bench. Everything you touch mid-set is big, and nothing needs typing.

Log a set with your thumb

Open the app and today's session is waiting, its targets worked out from your last sessions. Tap Start: reps and weight sit on two big steppers already set to the target. Lifted something else? Nudge them, then tap Done. Warm-up sets from your program come first.

Rest timer

Rest starts the moment you tap Done, with the time your program sets for that exercise. Add or take 15 seconds, see what's next, and keep the screen awake while you wait.

Plate calculator

One tap shows what goes on each side of the bar, worked out from your bar and the plates you actually own, in kg or lb. No mental arithmetic between sets.

History and progress

Every session is kept, set by set. Each exercise gets its own chart of estimated one-rep max and top set, so you can see the weeks add up.

Your programs

Keep several programs and switch the active one when your goals change. Bring in a new version when your AI revises the plan; earlier versions and the sessions trained with them stay in your history.

Offline and privacy

Works with no signal

Basement gyms and steel walls eat reception. Iron Oxide opens without a connection, keeps your sets on the phone and sends them as soon as it's back online. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Your data, in the EU

  • Hosted in the EU, in Frankfurt.
  • Sign in with a passkey or your Google account. No password to remember or leak.
  • Export your data or delete your account any time: those are your GDPR rights, and one-tap export and deletion are coming to Settings.
